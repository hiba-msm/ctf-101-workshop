# Getting Started

[Home](../README.md) › **Getting started** &nbsp;·&nbsp; Next: [CTF fundamentals →](CTF_FUNDAMENTALS.md)

You attended CTF 101 (or found this repository on your own). Here's how to go from zero to your first flag **today**, without installing anything.

---

## In 30 minutes: your first flag

1. **Bookmark three tools:** [CyberChef](https://gchq.github.io/CyberChef/) (decode anything), [dCode](https://www.dcode.fr/) (classic ciphers) and [Aperi'Solve](https://www.aperisolve.com/) (images).
2. **Create a free account** on [CyLab Security Academy](https://cylabacademy.org/) (formerly picoCTF). Old picoCTF accounts still work.
3. **Open the [challenge library](https://learn.cylabacademy.org/library)** and sort or filter by the easiest challenges in **General Skills** or **Cryptography**.
4. **Solve one.** Read the description twice, use the hints if you're stuck, and submit the flag.

That's it: you're a CTF player.

## Five habits of good CTF players

1. **Read carefully.** The title and description are clues.
2. **Take notes.** What you tried, what you found, what's left.
3. **Try the simple thing first.** View source, check metadata, decode the obvious.
4. **Time-box.** Stuck for 20+ minutes? Take a break, switch challenge or read a hint.
5. **Learn from writeups** after you've tried ([how](resources/WRITEUPS-AND-LEARNING.md)).

And one rule above all: **only hack what you have permission to hack**. See [the golden rule](CTF_FUNDAMENTALS.md#the-golden-rule-permission).
